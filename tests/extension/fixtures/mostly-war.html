Front line report

The war continued along the eastern front, where troops exchanged artillery fire through the night and officials reported new shelling near several villages close to the river crossing.

Talks stall

Negotiators failed to agree on a ceasefire for the third time this month, and both sides accused each other of preparing a new offensive while the invasion of the northern region went on.

Missile strikes

Missile strikes hit power stations across the country, and the army said its air defence had stopped most of them before they reached the city; the war has now lasted more than two years.

Refugees at the border

Thousands of people fleeing the war crossed the border on foot, carrying what they could, while volunteers handed out food and blankets at the crossing point late into the evening.
