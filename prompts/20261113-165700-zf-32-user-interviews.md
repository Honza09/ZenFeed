## Context

Sources: `zadani/zenfeed-final-proposal.md` §5 and §6, `zadani/VYZKUM-ZENFEED.md` section "Ověření potřeby", `docs/mvp.md` (§2 target user, §5/§7 test plan), `docs/testing/metodika-hodnoceni.md` (so the interviews do not duplicate the evaluation of the prototype, which is ZF-33/ZF-19).

Interviews are NOT a test of the prototype. Their purpose is to check the hypotheses from the research (they are hypotheses, not verified results, `docs/mvp.md` §2):
1. Some readers of the web feel overloaded by or want to avoid selected topics (war, political conflicts, death notices, disaster scenarios, violence, hostile tone).
2. They want a quick overview of a page and ordinary AI summaries ignore their preferences.
3. Hiding topics and a summary controlled by the same preferences is worth more than an ordinary summary.
4. Voice (historical-voice audio) has no direct evidence of need; verify it only as a question, do not lead.

Recruitment (proposal §5): adult Chrome users who read the web regularly, in Czech and in English, different kinds of pages, also people satisfied with current alternatives (manual summaries, Sider, reader modes, blockers); not only students; personal contacts and reading/productivity communities. Sample is small and not representative.

## Task
Prepare the material the interviewers need. You do NOT conduct, simulate or invent interviews, answers, quotes, participants or findings.

Create these files (Czech), nothing else:
1. `docs/testing/rozhovory/scenar.md`: interview guide, 30–40 minutes, semi-structured.
   - Opening and consent (what is recorded, how, that the participant may stop or skip any question).
   - Questions about the LAST concrete situation (what they read, why they needed a summary or wanted to skip something, what they did, what annoyed them, what they wanted to leave out, how they decided), never "would you like this idea?".
   - Questions for satisfied users of alternatives (why it is enough, what is missing).
   - Neutral probes ("co se stalo potom?", "jak často?", "jak jste to řešil/a?") and a list of leading questions NOT to ask, with a neutral rewrite for each.
   - A short, optional concept part at the end (one sentence what ZenFeed does, then what differs from their usual approach; ask for refusal reasons too). Voice only as one open question at the very end.
   - Mapping: for each question mark which hypothesis (1–4) it informs.
2. `docs/testing/rozhovory/nabor-a-souhlas.md`: recruitment screener (questions to include or exclude a person, quotas: CS/EN reading, kinds of pages, at least 1–2 satisfied users of alternatives, at most about half students), a short invitation text, a consent sheet (purpose, voluntary, recording optional, what is stored, anonymisation, deletion on request, contact is a team member but NOT a person's real data in the repo), and an anonymisation rule (participants are R01…R08, no names, e-mails, employers, exact ages, locations or page URLs that identify a person).
3. `docs/testing/rozhovory/zapis-sablona.md`: one-page note template per interview (ID, date, language, role in one general phrase, situation described, needs, pains, workarounds, wanted omissions, refusal reasons, quotes marked as quotes with the question that triggered them, interviewer's own interpretation kept in a separate field).
4. `docs/testing/rozhovory/zprava-sablona.md`: empty template of the final short report (table of hypotheses 1–4 vs. participants: supported / contradicted / no evidence, each cell with an evidence reference; recurring needs; examples of refusal; what it changes in `docs/mvp.md` as a PROPOSAL; limits: sample, selection, interviewer bias). Leave every result empty; the header says it is a template and findings are hypotheses.
5. One line each in `docs/README.md` and `docs/TESTING.md` linking `docs/testing/rozhovory/scenar.md`.
